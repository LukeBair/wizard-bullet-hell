How a run plays out

Every run lasts ten minutes. Most wizards don't.

You pick a spellbook and walk into the dark. Your spells fire on their own at whatever is closest. Every level-up hands you a choice, and every boss you beat moves the fight somewhere new.

  1. 0:00

    Pick a tome

    Start with one spellbook: Arcane Missile, Fireball, Frost Nova, Chain Lightning and more.

  2. 0:45

    The first swarm

    Dusk Bats pour in from every side. After this, the slimes bring friends.

  3. 2:30

    First boss

    Nothing else spawns until it falls. Win, and you gain another rune socket.

  4. 5:00

    New zone

    Each boss kill shifts the arena to a new zone with its own monsters. Again at 7:30.

  5. 10:00

    Witch's Hollow

    The final boss. Beat it and the run is yours.

Rune sockets

Build the spell, not the wizard.

The Grimoire

Lose the run. Keep the knowledge.

  • New spells, not bigger numbers

    Gold from each run unlocks new tomes for your Grimoire. Every run still starts from scratch, so a good run is always your doing.

  • Evolutions to earn

    Spells evolve up to tier III. Pull off feats during runs to unlock each evolution.

  • Short enough for a bus ride

    Ten minutes, portrait mode, one thumb.
